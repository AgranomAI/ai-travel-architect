## 11. Critical Warnings

- **Visa-free entry**: Polish citizens can stay in Japan up to 90 days without a visa.
- **Sakura is unpredictable**: Full bloom lasts 7–10 days. Rain can wash petals away in 48 hours. Book hotels with free cancellation and monitor forecasts.
- **Peak season**: Late March – early April is the most expensive period. Book 3–4 months in advance.
- **JR Pass price increase**: From October 1, 2026, price will rise. Buy early.
- **Cash**: Not everywhere accepts cards. Keep ¥20,000–30,000 cash.
- **Language barrier**: Tourist spots have English signs, but rural areas don’t. Download Google Translate with offline Japanese.

## 11. Critical Warnings

- **Visa-free entry**: Polish citizens can stay in Japan up to 90 days without a visa.
- **Sakura is unpredictable**: Full bloom lasts 7–10 days. Rain can wash petals away in 48 hours. Book hotels with free cancellation and monitor forecasts.
- **Peak season**: Late March – early April is the most expensive period. Book 3–4 months in advance.
- **JR Pass price increase**: From October 1, 2026, price will rise. Buy early.
- **Cash**: Not everywhere accepts cards. Keep ¥20,000–30,000 cash.
- **Language barrier**: Tourist spots have English signs, but rural areas don’t. Download Google Translate with offline Japanese.
- **Connection from Wrocław**: No direct flights to Tokyo. Expect at least one connection (Warsaw, Frankfurt, Munich, Helsinki). Total travel time 14–18 hours.
