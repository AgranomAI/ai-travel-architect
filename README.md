# ✈️ AI Travel Architect

### Turn any LLM into your personal travel agent.

AI Travel Architect is a universal prompt that turns ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other LLMs into a **personal travel architect**.

Instead of giving you a list of places to visit, it builds a complete trip:

* 🗺️ day-by-day itinerary
* ✈️ flights and transportation
* 🏨 accommodation
* 💰 budget
* 💡 money-saving ideas
* 📍 coordinates and maps
* 🎟️ activities and attractions
* 🚗 car rental considerations
* 🧳 practical travel checklist
* ⚠️ important warnings and Plan B

The goal is simple:

> **Tell the AI where you want to go. Let it figure out the trip.**

---

## 🚀 How to use

### 1. Copy the prompt

Open [`travel-architect.md`](./travel-architect.md) and copy the entire prompt. Or simply download entire file. 

### 2. Paste it into your LLM

It works with any AI model that allows you to provide a system prompt, custom instructions or a long initial prompt.

Examples:

* ChatGPT
* Claude
* Gemini
* other compatible LLMs

### 3. Tell it about your trip

For example:

> I want to spend 8 days in Japan in October.
> Flying from Amsterdam.
> Two adults.
> Budget €2,500.
> We love food, nature and small local places.

The Travel Architect will take it from there.

---

## 🧠 What makes it different?

Most travel prompts give you ideas.

This one is designed to **finish the job**.

The agent is instructed to avoid turning the conversation into a long questionnaire. It collects only critical information and then makes reasonable assumptions for everything else.

It can then build the trip from:

**idea → route → logistics → budget → bookings → maps → checklist**

It also explicitly distinguishes confirmed information from estimates and information that still needs verification.

---

## 🌍 Example

Try:

> Plan me a 7-day trip to Japan from Amsterdam.
> I want great food, nature and interesting neighbourhoods.
> Budget: €2,000 per person.

Then ask:

> Make it more local and less touristy.

Or:

> Reduce the budget by 15%.

Or:

> Replace Tokyo with another city.

The agent is designed to rebuild the itinerary when your requirements change rather than simply patching the previous answer.

---

## ⚠️ Important

AI-generated travel information can change.

Prices, availability, visa requirements, transportation schedules and booking conditions should always be checked on the relevant official or booking website before purchasing.

The prompt itself does **not** make a booking unless the AI platform you use has the necessary tools and permissions.
